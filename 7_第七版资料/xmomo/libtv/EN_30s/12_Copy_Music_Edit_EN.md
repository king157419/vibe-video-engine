## Wording to keep out

A US ad-industry review body told a competitor in this category to drop several of these claims in October 2026. Do not use: anti-aging, prevents wrinkles, acne, anti-bacterial, hypoallergenic, clinically proven, dermatologist recommended. The website's own product pages use some of these words; the film does not repeat them.

Lines this film uses instead: less friction than cotton · silk absorbs less than cotton · helps your skincare stay on your skin.

"OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100" may go on screen only with the brand's certificate number to hand. Without it, leave the line off.
